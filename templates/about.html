About ThingList

A simple, privacy-first way to track and organise your personal items. Export your data anytime — no lock-in, no ads.

Philosophy

Simple

Designed to keep track of your things with minimal friction.

Clean

Uncluttered pages to help you focus on what matters.

Open

Export and import your data at any time — you stay in control.

No ads

Your data will never be used to target advertisements.

Features

Multiple inventories

Organise items into separate inventories for clarity.

Custom field templates

Per-inventory field sets let you customize how items are displayed.

Search

Find items across all your inventories quickly.

Related items

Link related items to navigate your collections naturally.

Images

Attach photos to items for easier identification.

Visibility

Make inventories or items public or private as needed.
